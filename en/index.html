StradeMortali

StradeMortali is a civic technology and open data project by Jacopo Ottaviani, an Italian data journalist.

The data comes from the City of Rome (up to the latest available update).

The project is a prototype meant to spark a debate on the release and use of open data, and to raise public awareness of road safety.

Above all, it is meant as a decision-making tool to act on the most dangerous roads and intersections, supporting traffic interventions that improve citizens' safety.

To contact the author or report a data anomaly, the best way is LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/jacopo-ottaviani

Support and resources

Behind every row of this data there are people. If you or someone close to you has been involved in a road crash in Italy, free support services are available. Here you can also find resources to learn more.

Victims and families

  • ANIA Cares — Free psychological support for victims and families. Toll-free number 800 893 510, available 24/7.
  • AIFVS — Italian Association of Road Victims and their Families, with over 120 local branches.
  • Fondazione Michele Scarponi — Protection of vulnerable road users and support for victims' families.

Data and prevention

  • ISTAT — Official statistics on road crashes in Italy.
  • ACI — ACI-ISTAT studies and reports on road safety.
  • Fondazione ANIA — Road safety education and prevention.
  • Comune di Roma — Claims management and compensation procedures.
  • Roma Capitale — Open data on urban safety.
